CITY OF BROOKLYN DEVELOPMENT AREA — REZONING PACKAGE RMP-2031
Municipal Council Document Extract — Public Filing 2031-C

SECTION 4: BUILDING HEIGHT AND BULK REGULATIONS

4.1 Maximum Building Height, Fort Greene Overlay (FG-3)
The maximum permitted building height within the Fort Greene Overlay District (FG-3) is
increased from eighty-five (85) feet to one hundred twenty-seven (127) feet for any
residential or mixed-use development that dedicates no less than twenty percent (20%) of
total dwelling units to income-restricted affordable housing. This amendment applies to all
parcels bounded by DeKalb Avenue, Vanderbilt Avenue, and the Fort Greene Park perimeter.
Effective date: upon council ratification.

4.2 Ground-Floor Active Frontage Requirement, Clinton Hill Commercial Corridor
All new construction and substantial renovations fronting designated commercial corridors in
Clinton Hill must provide active, transparent ground-floor frontage occupying no less than
sixty percent (60%) of the building's street-facing facade. Blank walls, parking structures,
and storage facing the street are prohibited without a granted design variance.

4.3 Parking Minimum Reduction, Navy Yard Transit Corridor
For residential developments located within a one-quarter (1/4) mile walking distance of a
subway entrance in the Navy Yard Transit Corridor, the minimum required off-street parking
ratio is reduced from 0.5 spaces per unit to 0.2 spaces per unit. Developers may apply
remaining parking allocation toward bicycle storage or shared mobility infrastructure.

4.4 Affordable Housing Set-Aside, Bedford-Stuyvesant Growth Zone
Any residential development exceeding fifty (50) total dwelling units within the
Bedford-Stuyvesant Growth Zone must reserve no less than twenty-five percent (25%) of units
as permanently affordable, calculated at sixty percent (60%) of Area Median Income (AMI) or
below. Non-compliance will result in denial of certificate of occupancy.

SECTION 7: PUBLIC COMMENT SUMMARY (COUNCIL SESSION 2031-07-14)

Residents submitted 2,408 comments regarding the proposed amendments in Section 4.
Recurring themes, in order of frequency:
  1. Concern that height increases in FG-3 will accelerate rent pressure on adjacent
     rent-stabilized buildings, despite the affordability set-aside.
  2. Concern regarding construction-phase disruption: noise, dust, and multi-year
     timelines affecting small businesses along DeKalb Avenue.
  3. Requests for a formal shadow study before any height variance is finalized, citing
     impact on Fort Greene Park's afternoon sunlight exposure.
  4. Support for the parking minimum reduction in the Navy Yard corridor, citing existing
     transit access and a desire for less car-dependent development.
  5. Requests for clearer enforcement mechanisms behind the Bedford-Stuyvesant set-aside,
     citing past instances of developers paying in-lieu fees rather than building
     affordable units on-site.
